# Shizuo 2.24.6

The home page now focuses on finding and opening saved work.

- Open Inbox and New Board are in the top-right toolbar.
- The introduction, quick capture, onboarding steps, and duplicate Inbox card are removed.
- The system Inbox name is fixed and read-only; existing renamed Inboxes are corrected without removing cards.
- Ordinary board names remain editable.

Reload the unpacked extension from `chrome://extensions` to apply the update.
